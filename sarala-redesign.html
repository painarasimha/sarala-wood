Our Story

A home is remembered by its wood.

In 1986, Mrs. Sarala Hegde saw something others had overlooked: that the heritage carving traditions of our region belonged not only in temples and palaces, but in the homes of the families who lived around them.

What began as a small workshop in Nandalike, near Karkala, has grown into a studio of craftsmen who still work the way she believed in. We choose the timber, season it patiently and carve it by hand, so the grain and the detail age together.

Today we make doors and frames, windows, wardrobes, furniture and modular kitchens for homes, offices and places of worship across Karnataka and beyond.

The Craft

From log to heirloom.

Every piece passes through the same four hands-on stages. Nothing is rushed, because wood remembers how it was treated.

i.

Selecting

We hand-pick timber for grain, density and character, choosing each board for the piece it will become.

ii.

Seasoning

The wood is seasoned slowly so it stays stable through coastal monsoons and dry summers alike.

iii.

Carving

Master carvers bring out lotus, vine and temple motifs by hand, line by line, with chisel and mallet.

iv.

Finishing

Layered polishing deepens the natural tone and protects the surface without hiding the grain.
